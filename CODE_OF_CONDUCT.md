# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in Mindora a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Be welcoming.** Assume good faith. New contributors don't know the codebase yet.
- **Be specific.** "It doesn't work" is hard to act on. Logs, steps to reproduce, and expected vs. actual behavior help.
- **Disagree on ideas, not on people.** Critique the patch, not the person who wrote it.
- **Keep it private when it matters.** Security issues go to the maintainers, not public issues.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking corrective action. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Open a private contact through the project's security advisory page on GitHub, or reach the maintainer via the contact listed in their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
